DMCA & Photo Takedown Policy

How copyright holders can request action on content shown through this service.

How content on this service works

WhatsApp DP Downloader does not host a photo database. When someone searches a number, the profile picture is fetched live from WhatsApp and displayed to that user only. Results may be cached briefly in memory for performance and are then discarded. Images nevertheless remain the property of their owners, and we take copyright seriously.

Submitting a takedown request

If you are the copyright owner (or authorized agent) of a photo displayed through the service and want it restricted, open a takedown issue with the items below. Please do not post sensitive personal documents publicly — we'll contact you privately if anything further is needed:

  1. Your full name and contact email.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work and proof of ownership.
  3. The phone number (or URL) where the photo appears through our service.
  4. A good-faith statement that the use is unauthorized, and a declaration under penalty of perjury that your notice is accurate and you are the owner or authorized to act.
  5. Your physical or electronic signature.

Valid requests are typically actioned within 48 hours: we add the number to a blocklist so the photo is no longer served through our site, and we confirm by email.

Also report to WhatsApp

Because images originate from WhatsApp, the most effective way to stop unauthorized use is to report the account to WhatsApp directly (in-app: Profile → Report) or change your own profile-photo privacy setting (Settings → Privacy → Profile Photo → My Contacts or Nobody).

Counter-notices

If your number was blocklisted in error, reply with a counter-notice explaining why, and we'll review it.
