Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

WhatsApp DP Downloader ("we", "the service") is built to be private by default. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens when you use it.

How a lookup works (plain English)

  1. You type a phone number. Your browser sends only that number to our serverless lookup proxy (hosted on Vercel). We validate it and apply a short per-user rate limit.
  2. The proxy forwards the number to our lookup engine over an encrypted connection, using a secret key that never reaches your browser or any client-side code.
  3. The engine asks WhatsApp for the public profile picture linked to the number and downloads the image if one is available. Phone numbers are masked in our own logs (we keep an operational form like 947•••62, never the full number in log files).
  4. The image is delivered straight to your browser. Successful lookups may briefly stay in memory (a few hours) as an anti-lag cache, then are automatically discarded; negative results ("no photo") are cached for about 30 minutes.

We are intentionally conservative about over-promising: we do not claim total anonymity (that would be dishonest), we claim minimal retention — and the design above is exactly what that means.

1. What we collect

  • Lookup requests. When you search a number, that number is sent to our lookup backend, processed in real time, and discarded. We do not log searched numbers into any permanent database.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) processes standard request data (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention (rate limiting). These are short-lived operational logs.
  • No accounts, no analytics by default. We don't require sign-up and we don't profile visitors.

2. What we never do

  • Store the phone numbers you search or link them to you.
  • Keep a database of profile photos.
  • Notify the owner of a profile picture that it was viewed.
  • Sell or share any user data with third parties.

3. Caching

To keep the service fast and reduce load, a successful lookup may be cached in memory for a few hours, then automatically discarded. Cache entries are keyed only by the searched number and are never written to persistent storage by the application.

4. Cookies

This site does not set tracking cookies. Your browser may keep the site in its normal cache purely for performance.

5. Third parties

  • Vercel Inc. — hosting/CDN for this website (their privacy policy).
  • WhatsApp/Meta — profile photos are retrieved from WhatsApp's public endpoints; your number never leaves our backend, and we don't share anything with Meta beyond what the technical request itself requires.

6. Children's privacy

The service is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

7. Your rights

Because we don't store personal data about you, there's typically nothing to access or delete. If you believe your photo is being misused through the service, see the DMCA page. For anything else, contact us.

8. Advertising

To keep the service free, some pages show a small number of clearly-labeled sponsored links (marked "Advertisement"). Clicking one opens an advertiser's website in a new tab — that site is governed by its own privacy policy, not ours. We never transmit the phone numbers you search, or any images, to advertisers. Impression/click counts for these links are stored only in your own browser's local storage and never leave your device.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above reflects the current version.
